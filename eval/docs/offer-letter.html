Your date of joining will be 6 April 2026. Your primary place of work will be our Pune office. You will report to the Engineering Manager, Platform Engineering.

The Company follows a hybrid working model. Employees are expected to work from the office at least 3 days per week. Fully remote work is allowed only with written approval from your manager and HR.

Salary is paid monthly, on the last working day of each month, by bank transfer.

3. Joining Bonus

You will receive a one-time joining bonus of Rs. 1,00,000, paid with your first month's salary. If you resign or are terminated for cause within 12 months of your joining date, you must repay the full joining bonus to the Company.

4. Relocation Support

If you are moving to Pune from another city, the Company will reimburse actual relocation expenses up to Rs. 50,000 against original bills. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of your joining date. The Company will also provide company-paid hotel accommodation for up to 15 days after joining.

5. Probation

You will be on probation for a period of 6 months from your date of joining. The probation period may be extended by up to 3 more months if your performance needs improvement. You will be informed in writing when your employment is confirmed.

6. Notice Period

During probation, either you or the Company may end your employment by giving 15 days' written notice. After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days. The Company may, at its sole discretion, accept payment of basic salary in lieu of the notice period.

7. Working Hours

The standard working week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. You may occasionally be part of an on-call rotation; on-call weeks are compensated at Rs. 4,000 per week.

8. Leave

You are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month. Up to 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next year. In addition, you get 12 days of sick leave and 10 public holidays per year as per the Company holiday list. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

Parental leave is available as per the Company policy and applicable law: 26 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.

9. Benefits

You and your dependants (spouse, up to two children and parents) will be covered under the Company group health insurance with a sum insured of Rs. 5,00,000 per year. Group term life insurance cover is three times your annual fixed pay. You will also receive a yearly learning budget of Rs. 30,000 for courses, books and conferences.

The Company provides a laptop for work. The laptop remains Company property and must be returned on your last working day.

10. Gratuity and Provident Fund

You will be a member of the Employees' Provident Fund. Gratuity will be paid as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, which normally requires 5 years of continuous service.

11. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

You must keep all Company information confidential during and after your employment. All work you create in the course of your employment belongs to the Company. You must sign the Company's confidentiality and invention assignment agreement on your joining date.

12. Outside Work

You must not take up any other paid employment while working for the Company without written permission from HR. Unpaid open-source contributions are allowed if they do not use Company code or confidential information.

13. Non-Solicitation

For 12 months after leaving the Company, you must not directly persuade any Company employee to leave the Company, and must not solicit any client you worked with during your last 12 months of employment.

14. Background Verification

This offer is subject to satisfactory background verification, including education and previous employment checks. If any information you provided is found to be false, the Company may withdraw this offer or end your employment.

15. Documents Required on Joining

Please bring your educational certificates, relieving letters from previous employers, your last three salary slips, and two passport-size photographs.

16. Acceptance

This offer is valid until 17 March 2026. Please sign and return a copy of this letter by that date to confirm your acceptance. If we do not receive your acceptance by then, this offer will lapse.

We look forward to welcoming you to Northwind Orbit.
